Empirical evidence concerns a specified study and its tested conditions. Editorial convention is practical writing judgement, not a measured detector rule. House preferences include Australian English, no semicolons in authored headings and Damian's Teamwork Desk conventions. Hypotheses about sample features remain tentative and context-specific. Do not promote any category into another.

Prefer primary papers for their results and sound synthesis for evaluating a body of evidence. Titles, inherited citations, detector demonstrations and agreement between models are insufficient. ## Inspected evidence

Accessed 14 October 2026. These are concise, source-bounded paraphrases, not claims about this package's performance.

### Personal style approximation

Wang, Tripto, Park, Li and Zhou, [Catch Me If You Can? Not Yet: LLMs Still Struggle to Imitate the Implicit Writing Styles of Everyday Authors](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.532.pdf), Findings of EMNLP, November 2025. Inspected the proceedings PDF, abstract, methodology and limitations.

The tested few-shot models approximated style in news and email more successfully than nuanced informal blog/forum style. Evaluation used computational authorship, style and detection measures on English datasets within the same genre. It did not include large-scale human assessment or test cross-genre transfer. This supports caution about exact imitation, not a promise that this skill replicates voice, or universal superiority of distilled style instructions over direct samples.

### Detector robustness

Dugan and colleagues, [RAID: A Shared Benchmark for Robust Evaluation of Machine-Generated Text Detectors](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.674.pdf), ACL, August 2024. Inspected the proceedings PDF, abstract and limitations.

In the evaluated benchmark, tested detectors were sensitive to adversarial attacks, sampling variation and unseen generators. The dataset is finite, multilingual coverage is limited and model changes require maintenance. This does not establish an individual text's authorship, editing quality, a punctuation rule, or universal detector defeat. No fixed decoding settings or detector-optimised rewriting method is adopted here.

### Typography counterexample

Microsoft Support, [Smart quotes in Word and PowerPoint](https://support.microsoft.com/en-au/word/smart-quotes-in-word-and-powerpoint), current support page, no publication/update date shown in the inspected content. Word and PowerPoint can automatically convert typed straight quotes to curly quotes. Our inference: curly quotation marks alone cannot establish AI authorship. This is a software feature counterexample, not a detector accuracy study or a claim about how a particular text was typed.
