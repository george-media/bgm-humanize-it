## Candidate identity and evidence boundary

I checked the frozen snapshot at `outputs/skill-build-evidence/candidates/v1` against `candidate-v1-manifest.json`; all six SHA-256 values match. A recursive comparison with the current candidate shows one change only: `SKILL.md`, line 34, replaces “slang” with “artificial slang”. The five remaining package files are byte-identical.

The execution provenance says candidate hashes were captured after output generation and the originally read entrypoint was reconstructed by reversing that qualification change, then compared with the frozen snapshot. My manifest check corroborates the saved snapshot's identity, not a contemporaneous hash at the worker's initial read. The v1 results should be reported as v1 results. They do not constitute fresh execution of the amended entrypoint, and these requests do not directly exercise user-authorised natural slang.

The saved reference lists record candidate reads. They are not a complete tool-action trace, automatic-discovery log or proof that another skill was unloaded. The absence of errors in this finite set must not be generalised into guaranteed fidelity or detector performance.
