# Editing and voice

Read for substantial revision, sample-led work or a difficult style decision. These are editorial choices, not a checklist of mandatory removals. The current brief and useful original language take precedence over a default preference for terse prose.

## Intervention

For a light edit, fix demonstrated errors and local ambiguities while retaining structure, register and useful wording. Already-clear text may need no change. For a substantial revision, reorganise where the reader benefits, but keep the author's argument, evidence, reservations and commitments. For a new draft, build from the supplied material rather than inferring experiences or a position the author never expressed.

An optional brief can contain purpose, audience and what they know, main point or action, format, tone/register, length, house style and edit scope. Treat these as helpful context, not prerequisites. Ask only questions that affect fidelity or usefulness. When an essential event date or venue is absent, a send-ready invitation needs clarification. A self-contained abstract paragraph needs no invented number or compulsory concrete example. If partial drafting helps, use supplied facts and label any permitted placeholder; clearly distinguish partial work from final copy.

## Samples

Read representative samples for vocabulary, formality, directness, rhythm, paragraph movement, openings, punctuation habits, recurring phrasing and transitions. Consider whether a feature belongs to this writer, the sample's topic or its destination. Use tentative observations, not quotas or universal claims. A formal sample does not require formal wording in a requested friendly note. A single fragment does not justify fragmenting every paragraph.

Apply relevant features in fresh wording. Do not copy a distinctive catchphrase or transfer sample-only dates, expertise or personal history without the user's permission. Authorised quotation or approved boilerplate can be reused faithfully. Wording already in the edited draft is legitimate material; overlap with a sample neither requires its deletion nor proves authorship. Preserve the draft's useful jokes, empathy, repeated phrasing and emphasis. Do not inflate a mild tic into caricature or flatten a warm voice into corporate shorthand.

## Meaning and evidence

Track numbers and their denominators, time periods, actors, commitments and conditional language. If six of twelve responses is called 75%, identify the discrepancy and make any calculation correction transparent. Do not guess that the numerator was supposed to be nine. A citation documenting response rate does not also support an adjacent causal claim about effectiveness. Moving sentences must not widen citation scope.

Keep meaningful uncertainty and objections. Remove a hedge only if its absence leaves the same degree of certainty. Preserve a genuine comparison and its conditions: a faster method may use more memory, and suitability may depend on workload. Challenge a weak inference without changing the author's stance or inventing provocative conclusions.

Preserving input is not endorsing an error. Identify material contradictions or unsupported claims. A reliable correction can be applied with a brief transparent note or the requested change format. If uncertainty blocks faithful final copy, clarify or clearly identify the unresolved issue according to the destination. A copy-only instruction does not require delivering a consequential error as fact.

For requested verification, inspect supporting sources directly. Use primary evidence for original factual claims and sound synthesis for a body of evidence. Follow independently applicable current-verification requirements. State unavailable tools, inaccessible sources and narrower evidence honestly. An editor's approval, another model's agreement or a simulated audience reaction does not verify a fact or measure actual reader response.

## Language and structure

Prefer familiar words when equally precise. Keep established disciplinary terms, including robust regression, leverage scores and comprehensive test suites. Explain terms only as much as this audience needs. Maintain one stable name for a referent and use clear pronouns rather than cycling synonyms to create artificial variety.

Simplify avoidable verbal wrappers and nominalisations when the subject and meaning survive. Replace empty significance with a supported concrete assertion. Enthusiasm is legitimate in promotional writing if it serves the brief and evidence. A vague attribution needs a real source or transparent qualification; do not invent an expert or quietly delete a central disputed assertion.

Give paragraphs a clear purpose appropriate to the format. A narrative may begin with scene or context; an update may lead with an action. Neither opening is compulsory. Use active voice to clarify responsibility and passive voice for useful emphasis or an unknown actor. Keep logical connections, necessary list items, headings, ordered instructions and reader-useful conclusions. Repetition, rhetorical questions, anaphora, triads, chiasmus, mirrored clauses, reveal pivots and aphoristic endings can serve meaning or voice. Assess their purpose and frequency, not their mere presence.

Remove irrelevant acknowledgements, flattery and canned sign-offs from deliverable copy while retaining suitable courtesy, useful empathy and required wording. Short async writing need not be disordered, lowercase or slang-filled. Personal address and interruptions need a communicative reason, not an authenticity quota.

## Stop and reread

Sentence-length ranges, standard-deviation targets, mid-band caps, fragment intervals, dash/colon limits, fixed paragraph sizes and novelty counts are not universal requirements. Follow a feasible user-specified measurable preference as house style, without calling it authorship science. If it conflicts with factual fidelity or literal wording, resolve the conflict rather than silently breaking either.

Do not manufacture deliberate misfires, mistakes, poor links, off-topic additions, fake self-corrections or forced unresolved endings. Do not force lowercase, arbitrary asymmetry or disfluency quotas as authenticity tests. No residual-pattern gate overrides meaning, genre or voice. Avoid repeated paraphrase and detector-driven variant selection. Similar self-rewrites do not establish an individual text's authorship or probability status. Do not use fixed generation settings, homoglyph changes or watermark tricks to evade detection.

After substantial shortening, check what was lost, including explanation, empathy and humour. A neutral shortening note is optional when useful or requested; length alone cannot establish puffery. Reread actual final copy for fidelity and citation alignment, comprehension and structure, then voice and house style. Recheck changed passages and every legitimate exception. Stop once the requested improvement is achieved.
