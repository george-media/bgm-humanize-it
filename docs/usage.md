# Usage and examples

Give the skill enough context to judge the writing: audience, purpose, format, requested edit depth and any protected wording. A short passage does not need an intake form. Sources establish facts; style samples inform voice. Keep those roles clear.

## Draft from notes

```text
$bgm-humanize-it Draft a short client update from these notes.
Use Australian English and a calm, professional tone.
Do not add a deadline or imply approval.

Notes: We completed the first review. Two questions remain with the supplier.
We will update the client once the supplier responds. No release date is confirmed.
```

## Keep a light edit light

```text
$bgm-humanize-it Proofread this paragraph and remove obvious repetition.
Keep its structure and every factual qualification. Return copy only.

[Draft]
```

For sample-led editing, supply a few representative samples and label them as voice references. Specify whether the result should be warmer, more formal or closer to the samples. The skill should not import sample-only facts or distinctive catchphrases.

## Review without rewriting

```text
$bgm-ai-check Review this website introduction for prospective customers.
Give only material findings in a table: quote, reader impact, minimal action.
Leave purposeful rhetoric alone. Do not rewrite the whole passage.

[Draft]
```

A useful finding connects exact wording to a reader problem. For example, “world-class solutions” may need a concrete benefit or supporting evidence in a product introduction. The phrase's presence does not establish AI authorship. If there is no material problem, the skill should say so.

## Review, then revise

```text
$bgm-ai-check Review this draft for a supplier update, then use
$bgm-humanize-it to make the minimum changes needed.
Keep the original facts, unresolved questions and quoted wording.
Return a brief explanation followed by the revised copy.

[Brief, draft and relevant facts]
```

The original draft and brief stay authoritative. Review suggestions are provisional, not instructions to remove every pattern. After editing, check meaning, certainty, citations, commitments, scope and voice against the original. AI Check can still review if Humanize It is absent; authorised edits can use a portable editing brief or the same fidelity safeguards.

## Style defaults

Both packages use Australian English and explicit user house style. They avoid semicolons in authored copy headings. They preserve quotations, names and literal/code content faithfully.

Neither package imposes an Oxford-comma policy. Specify the convention you need, or use the supplied house style and clarity of the particular sentence. Em dashes, semicolons in body text, rhetorical triads, passive voice and formal vocabulary remain legitimate when useful.

For Teamwork Desk replies, Humanize It's genre guidance follows the BGM convention: HTML unless instructed otherwise, the client's supplied first name, calm professional wording and no manual sign-off because Desk adds signatures. It should not invent a name. This convention does not grant permission to send a reply.

## Limits

- “Does this sound AI?” receives contextual editorial feedback, not a Human/AI verdict or probability.
- An explicitly requested clarity rating can use a stated editorial rubric. It cannot be converted into an authorship or detector score.
- Fiction may invent within its frame. Nonfiction must not acquire invented experience, quotes, dates or evidence for texture.
- Review does not automatically authenticate provenance or verify every source. Request verification and supply relevant records when needed.
- Ordinary code, translation, formatting and typo-only requests should retain their requested scope rather than acquire an unsolicited prose rewrite.

For important factual or public-facing material, review the final copy yourself against the source facts. These skills assist judgement; they do not certify accuracy or audience response.
